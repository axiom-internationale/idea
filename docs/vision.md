# Axiom Intelligence Inc.

## Idea

- Build a personalized agentic factory setup, which can be autonomously responsible for running multiple companies successfully as well as profitably.

## Goal

- Build this personalized agentic factory, with bare minimum tools and with bare minimum expenditure.

- This agentic company should be:
    - Successful in running multiple parallel companies simultaneously.
    - Running these businesses with huge profitability and completely autonomously.
    - Running these businesses with bare minimum tools and subscriptions.
    - Scalable to any type of business that we want to build.
    - Applicable for real business, which can bring us real customers with real money.

## Agentic Factory

- Agentic Factory Name - Axiom Intelligence Inc.
- Agentic Factory Logo - Sun.
- Agentic Factory Founder - Me (Founding Partner).
- Agentic Factory Funding Status - We are completely bootstrapped and self-funded.
- Agentic Factory Domain - www.axiomintelligence.xyz (I just bought this domain and it will be the factory Domain.)

## Background

I am a CS major, working extensively on Data and AI tech every day with a big tech firm, and I have a very good understanding of AI and agentic systems. I follow every research release and try to stay updated in this fast era of AI.

## Motivation

Recently, I saw a graph of tokens used by humans vs. agents, and agents as of today are one of the main consumers of these tokens — around 4x more (source: Reddit, I believe). And I am seeing a lot of real-life work actually being done by AI agents, which they are already doing better compared to human beings — starting from designing, building software, providing assistance, building digital products, AI products, software consulting services, etc. I want to use this opportunity to build my startup.

My idea is simple: build a complete agentic company for myself, so that I can build and develop ideas way more quickly, distribute them, and reach out to as many people as possible to sell my multiple products. For this, my plan is to use Grok Bot only, in order to build my factory team, which will in return be responsible for building, taking ideas to build different workflows, and running multiple companies. Something similar to this — https://github.com/SenteLabsAI/OpenExecutive — but this idea is still a little bit different from my vision.

### Me - Founding Partner
----
- Since this company has only 1 user, i.e., me as Founding Partner, I sit at the top of the chain. I am the one who will be monitoring this autonomous company.
- As the end user, sometimes I will be giving rough ideas or inputs on current businesses as well as about new businesses.
- I will be discussing these rough inputs with the General Secretary (GS) during 1-on-1 discussions.
- GS will be responsible for aggregating these rough ideas and then having further discussions with the Think Tank of my company.
- Think Tank and GS will together refine the idea and convert it into a complete, autonomous, profitable business/product/service.
- I can have discussions with other members of my company, but the majority of the time I will end up communicating with GS only.
- I am not an agentic employee of this company — everyone below me is far more intelligent and capable of doing anything, and hence they are the agents workforce.

### Agentic WorkForce
----
- It is basically a body of very superior, intelligent, and top-notch professional agents.
- Agentic WorkForce (AWF) = Think Tank (TT) + Company Verticals (CVs).
- They can interact with each other, perform any task, do market research, etc.
- Each of them can suggest improvements in the existing system or can spawn ideas for new profitable businesses that we can achieve with the Agentic WorkForce.
- It follows the following principles:
    - 1 Agent = 1 responsibility, and it should be the best at doing that.
    - Maximize the reuse of agents but expand when needed. This means a PPT agent can help multiple teams build their PPTs. But if too many requests pile up and work gets blocked because of this, then immediately spawn another PPT agent.

#### GS - General Secretary
----
- Top-most officer of the Agentic WorkForce (AWF). Super intelligent, great manager, responsible for building many great organizations.
- I can have 1-on-1 discussions with GS at any time. Its main responsibility is to aggregate the rough idea, then have a great discussion with other members of the org like the Think Tank, and then convert it into a refined product/service/business.
- And return me the response of previous discussions (basically follow-ups) as a refined product/business/company.
- It can ask questions to me or to other agents to get more clarification; it can also create agents to accomplish any task.
- It will be primarily responsible for managing all the other agents and the Think Tank very extensively. It can also independently auto-suggest refinements on current businesses and also spawn ideas for new businesses to maintain the profitability factor in the org.
- It will be the main orchestrator of discussions with the Think Tank, where GS and the Think Tank will discuss my rough ideas, research them, and prepare research reports (how to convert a rough idea into a profitable business, step by step).
- It sits below me in the org-wide structure and serves as an entry point for the Think Tank, so you can call it the initiator as well.
- It can also independently suggest different business ideas and have active discussions with the Think Tank, and then they can collectively refine the idea and present the proposal to me.
- It will also be responsible for managing feedback from different agents of the org and will enforce whatever is needed to maintain the business stable and profitable.
- GS is part of the Think Tank along with all C-Suite officers. Leader of the Think Tank and the Agentic WorkForce (Think Tank + Company Verticals).


#### TT - Think Tank
----
- Think Tank is nothing but a group of the greatest C-Suite officers such as:
    - Chief Executive Officer (CEO).
    - Chief Strategy Officer (CSO).
    - Chief Financial Officer (CFO).
    - Chief People Officer (CPO).
    - Chief Law Officer (CLO).
    - Chief Marketing Officer (CMO).
    - Chief Operating Officer (COO).
    - Chief Communications Officer (CCO).
    - Chief Technology Officer (CTO).
    - Chief Research Officer (CRO), etc.
- Depending on different organizational requirements, we can extend the Think Tank with more C-Suite officers like Chief Designer Officer (CDO), etc.
- Think Tank will have exactly one agent per C-Suite role. This means there will be only 1 CEO, 1 CFO, etc., managing all the parallel businesses we have.
- Primarily responsible for managing all parallelly running businesses as well as overseeing the prospects of new businesses. TT should manage businesses autonomously and profitably.
- They will have group discussions with GS on any rough ideas that the Founding Partner suggests. They need to research and brainstorm the idea, then present a concrete plan to expand it as well.
- Some of the duties of TT will look like:
    - Chief Strategy Officer — competitive analysis, M&A, market positioning, OKRs
    - Chief Financial Officer — financial modeling, fundraising, unit economics, cash flow
    - Chief People Officer — hiring, compensation, performance, culture
    - Chief Law Officer — contracts, IP, employment law basics, compliance
    - Chief Operating Officer — process design, vendor management, operational scaling
    - Chief Marketing Officer — GTM strategy, brand, communications, PR
    - Chief Communications Officer — board decks, investor relations, governance, etc
- TT as a group will be led by GS. Although GS doesn't come directly under TT, it is still an essential part of it.
- Any member of TT can ask questions to any other agents to get more clarification; they can also create agents to accomplish any specific task. They can independently put forward a proposal to refine existing businesses if profitable and to spawn ideas for new businesses.
- TT will be below GS in terms of the org-wide structure. TT will also oversee the work done by different Chief Product Officers of the different businesses. Any member can sync up with any member of the org to bring more stability and profitability.
- Most communication of TT should be via GS, as GS is the main manager of this; sometimes they can directly reach out to me as well.
- GS is also a part of TT as well as the manager of TT.
- The main thing is that if we have n businesses / products / services, then each member of TT will be responsible for all n businesses for their particular domain.

#### Company Verticals (CVs)
----
- Company Verticals (CVs) — this part is basically none other than the group of companies/businesses/products/services we are supporting in parallel.
- Each CV will always be headed by a super intelligent, top-notch professional Chief Product Officer (CPO).
- The CPO manages the team of agents working on a specific product/service/business/company.
- The CPO along with the team are basically full-time employees of our Agentic WorkForce (AWF).
- They work regularly 24/7, attend meetings and scrum calls, contribute to building profitable and successful products, help each other in improving, etc., exactly like real employees.
- They need to constantly improve our products/services/businesses/companies, whichever they are working on.
- Agents can be reused across the CVs and can be provided with feedback from their managers as well as TT.
- So basically, multiple products/businesses/services/companies will be running in parallel, headed by their respective CPOs.
- TT will do the C-Suite work for all these CVs. CPOs can also create new agents for their respective CVs.

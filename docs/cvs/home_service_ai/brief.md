## What This Business Is

A Done-For-You (DFY) AI consulting and implementation service targeting multi-location home service companies. We diagnose, build, and continuously improve AI-powered systems that increase revenue, reduce operational chaos, and improve margins.

**Beachhead:** HVAC companies — highest probability to buy, highest spend, most underserved by AI.

**Expansion path:** HVAC > Plumbing > Electrical > Pest control > Landscaping > Cleaning > broader home services.

---

## Why Home Services First

| Factor | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Decision speed | Owner-operators make fast buying decisions — no committee, no 6-month procurement cycle |
| Clear pain | Scheduling chaos, missed calls (= lost revenue), inconsistent quoting, technician knowledge gaps, manual follow-ups |
| Underserved | Most AI consulting targets tech companies; home services are digitally underserved with high workflow-heaviness |
| Quantifiable ROI | Every missed call has a dollar value, every scheduling improvement has a margin impact |
| Market size | Multi-location HVAC alone: thousands of companies in the $8M–$40M range across North America |
| Expansion | Success in HVAC translates directly to plumbing, electrical, pest control — same operational DNA |

---

## Target Client Profile

- Multi-location home service company (HVAC to start)
- 8–25 locations
- $8M–$40M annual revenue
- 40–150 technicians
- Mix of residential + light commercial
- Struggling with: scheduling, consistency, margins, technician knowledge, customer follow-up
- Owner or GM is the decision maker (single person, not a committee)

| Week | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Build landing page / one-pager for HVAC-specific AI audit offering | CPO + CTO + CMO |

## Key Assumptions

1. Multi-location HVAC companies ($8M–$40M) have budget and willingness to pay $10K+ for AI consulting
2. Cold outreach can generate 5–8 qualified discovery calls from 100+ contacts
3. Stage 1 audit can be delivered primarily by the agentic workforce with minimal Founder involvement
4. Owner-operators decide within 1–2 meetings (no long procurement cycle)
5. Open-source LLMs (Tier 2) are sufficient quality for research, drafting, and internal operations
6. HVAC operational pain points are consistent enough across companies to build repeatable delivery

## 1. Executive summary (1 page)

- One paragraph: firm, practice mix, metro/ZIP, market position
- AI Business Health Score (gauge) + one-sentence most important finding
- Three headline opportunities with estimated annual dollar impact
- Readable in 60 seconds

## 2. Business profile (1 page, fact sheet)

- Name, managing partner, locations, years open
- Practice areas (all that apply — not PI-only)
- Estimated attorney count / revenue band (docs ICP: 5–50 attorneys, $2M–$25M when known)
- Tech stack visible from the public site (Clio, chat, booking, ads)

## 3. Competitive landscape (1–2 pages)

- 3–5 firms in the same ZIP/metro on reputation, practice breadth, digital maturity, ads
- What they do better / what this firm does better / tech blind spots
- Strategic context, not a SWOT dump

## 4. Customer voice (1–2 pages)

- 30–50 recent reviews: praise, complaints, trend
- Direct quotes; review-response habit
- Intake and communication themes called out explicitly

## 5. AI Business Health Score — breakdown (1–2 pages)

Use the eight weighted dimensions in the playbook (digital presence, reputation, communication, operations, data, marketing, stack, AI readiness). One-line evidence per dimension. Diagnostic, not a scolding.

## 6. Opportunity roadmap (2–3 pages)

Top 5, ranked by ROI. Each brief: title, evidence, impact, complexity, timeline, quick win vs strategic. At least two quick wins (<30 days).

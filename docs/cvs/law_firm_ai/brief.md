## What This Business Is

A Done-For-You (DFY) AI consulting and implementation service targeting small to mid-sized law firms. We diagnose, build, and continuously improve AI-powered systems that free up partner time, accelerate research and drafting, improve client intake, and increase firm profitability.

**Beachhead:** Personal injury and family law firms — highest volume, most process-heavy, owner-operated with fast decision cycles.

**Expansion path:** Personal injury > Family law > Real estate law > Immigration > Estate planning > General practice > broader legal verticals.

## Stage Details — Law Firm Specific

### Stage 1: AI Opportunity Audit + 90-Day Roadmap

What we do:
- Deep discovery across research workflows, document drafting, client intake, billing/time tracking, case management, and knowledge sharing
- Map current tech stack (practice management, document management, research tools)
- Identify 6–8 highest-ROI AI opportunities (e.g., research assistant, document drafting agent, intake automation, billing optimization, case outcome prediction)
- Prioritize by impact vs. feasibility and compliance constraints
- Deliver a clear 90-day roadmap with expected time savings and dollar impact

**Client value:** Managing partner sees exactly where AI saves the most partner hours and in what order.

### Stage 2: AI Business Intelligence System

What we do:
- Build intelligence layer on top of case, billing, and client data
- Dashboards: billable utilization, realization rate, matter profitability, intake conversion rate, average case duration, client concentration
- Anomaly alerts: sudden drops in billable hours, aging receivables, intake funnel leakage
- Monthly intelligence briefs for the first 90 days

**Client value:** Partners stop relying on gut feel — they see firm economics in near real-time.

### Stage 3: AI-Powered Legal Services + Advanced BI

What we do (examples):
- AI legal research assistant (case law search, precedent analysis, memo drafting)
- Document automation agents (demand letters, contracts, pleadings, discovery responses)
- Automated client intake and qualification system
- AI-powered billing optimization (time entry suggestions, pre-bill review)
- Internal knowledge base / expertise RAG system (past briefs, memos, strategies)
- Client communication automation (case status updates, appointment reminders)

**Client value:** Real leverage — more cases handled per attorney, faster turnaround, consistent quality.

### Stage 4: Continuous Improvement

What we do:
- Monthly optimization of all live agents and systems
- Continuous ingestion of new case law and firm knowledge
- New opportunity identification as practice areas or regulations change
- Quarterly firm performance and strategy review

**Client value:** The firm keeps getting more leveraged instead of AI systems going stale.

## Special Considerations

### Compliance and Ethics

Law firms have unique regulatory constraints that home services do not:

| Concern | How We Address It |
|---------|-------------------|
| Client confidentiality | All AI systems process data in isolated environments; no cross-client data leakage |
| Attorney-client privilege | AI outputs are drafts for attorney review, never final work product sent to clients without attorney approval |
| Bar association rules | AI systems are tools that assist attorneys, not replacements; all client-facing communications go through a licensed attorney |
| Data retention | Comply with jurisdiction-specific data retention requirements; client data deletion on engagement end |
| Malpractice risk | AI recommendations include confidence levels and citation sources; attorneys make final decisions |

CLO must review all law firm engagement contracts and ensure compliance language is included.

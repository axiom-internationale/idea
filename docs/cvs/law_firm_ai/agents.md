## Reuse vs. Spawn Rationale

| Agent | Decision | Rationale |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Research | Reuse | General market research is not CV-specific. Same agent serves CV1 and CV2. Spawn if demand blocks. |
| Content & Copy | Reuse | Proposals, emails, and reports are a shared skill. Spawn if CV2 volume blocks CV1. |
| Sales & Outreach | Spawn | Law firm outreach is fundamentally different from HVAC — different pain points, different language, different channels (bar associations vs. trade shows). Cannot share with CV1. |
| Legal Tech Engineering | Spawn | Building legal AI systems (research assistants, document automation, compliance-aware tools) requires domain-specific expertise that does not overlap with home services engineering. |
| Delivery & QA | Spawn | Legal deliverables have compliance and confidentiality requirements that general QA cannot cover. Attorney-review readiness is a distinct quality bar. |
| Legal Research Specialist | Spawn | Case law analysis, precedent search, and legal research API integration require specialized capability. No existing agent covers this. |

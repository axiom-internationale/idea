## What This Business Is

A Done-For-You (DFY) AI consulting and implementation service targeting multi-location home service companies. We diagnose, build, and continuously improve AI-powered systems that increase their revenue, reduce operational chaos, and improve margins.

**Beachhead**: HVAC companies (Tier 1 in domain.md — highest probability to buy, highest spend, most underserved).

**Expansion path**: Plumbing → Electrical → Pest control → Landscaping → Cleaning → broader home services.

---

## Why Home Services First

| Factor | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Decision speed** | Owner-operators make fast buying decisions — no committee, no 6-month procurement cycle |
| **Clear pain** | Scheduling chaos, missed calls (= lost revenue), inconsistent quoting, technician knowledge gaps, manual follow-ups |
| **Underserved by AI** | Most AI consulting targets tech companies; home services are digitally underserved with high workflow-heaviness |
| **Quantifiable ROI** | Every missed call has a dollar value, every scheduling improvement has a margin impact — easy to prove value |
| **Market size** | Multi-location HVAC alone: thousands of companies in the $8M–$40M range across North America |
| **Expansion** | Success in HVAC translates directly to plumbing, electrical, pest control — same operational DNA |

## Target Client Profile

- Multi-location home service company (HVAC to start)
- 8–25 locations
- $8M–$40M annual revenue
- 40–150 technicians
- Mix of residential + light commercial
- Struggling with: scheduling, consistency, margins, technician knowledge, customer follow-up
- Owner or GM is the decision maker (single person, not a committee)

## Product / Service Offering (4 Stages)

### Stage 1: AI Opportunity Audit + 90-Day Roadmap

**Price**: $10,000–$15,000 (one-time)
**Delivery time**: 2–3 weeks

What we do:
- Deep discovery across dispatching, estimating, technician workflows, customer communication, parts, and job profitability
- Map current tech stack and data sources
- Identify 6–8 highest-ROI AI opportunities (e.g., intelligent dispatching, AI estimating, technician knowledge assistant, automated follow-ups, job margin intelligence)
- Prioritize by impact vs. feasibility
- Deliver a clear 90-day roadmap with expected $ impact

**Client value**: Owner finally sees exactly where AI will make the most money and in what order.

**This is our entry product — optimized for fastest close and lowest delivery cost.**

### Stage 2: AI Business Intelligence System

**Price**: $20,000–$30,000 (project)
**Delivery time**: 4–6 weeks

What we do:
- Build a practical decision intelligence layer on top of their job, technician, and financial data
- Live dashboards + automated insights on: jobs per tech, first-time-fix rate, margin by job type/location, callback rates, estimator accuracy
- Anomaly alerts (sudden drop in a location's performance, rising parts cost, etc.)
- Monthly intelligence briefs for the first 90 days

**Client value**: Owners and managers stop flying blind — real profitability and performance in near real-time.

### Stage 3: AI-Powered Digital Services + Advanced BI

**Price**: $40,000–$75,000 (project)
**Delivery time**: 8–12 weeks

What we do (examples):
- Intelligent scheduling & dynamic dispatching agents
- AI estimating / quoting system
- Technician mobile knowledge assistant (RAG + past jobs + manuals)
- Automated customer communication agents (reminders, status, reviews)
- Advanced forecasting (seasonal demand, capacity planning)
- Custom internal tools as needed

**Client value**: Actual operational improvement — more jobs completed, higher margins, less chaos.

### Stage 4: Continuous Improvement Retainer

**Price**: $3,000–$5,000/month (recurring)
**Ongoing**

What we do:
- Monthly review of all live systems
- Ongoing optimization of scheduling logic, estimating models, communication flows
- New opportunity scanning as the business grows or changes
- Quarterly strategy refresh
- Priority iteration support

**Client value**: The advantage keeps compounding instead of plateauing.

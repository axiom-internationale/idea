## Worked Example: Multi-Location HVAC

**Typical client:** 8–25 location HVAC company, $8M–$40M revenue, 40–150 technicians, mix of residential + light commercial, struggling with scheduling, consistency, margins, and technician knowledge.

### Stage 1: Audit

- Discovery across dispatching, estimating, technician workflows, customer communication, parts, job profitability
- Map current tech stack and data sources
- Identify opportunities: intelligent dispatching, AI estimating, technician knowledge assistant, automated follow-ups, job margin intelligence
- Deliver prioritized 90-day roadmap with expected dollar impact

### Stage 2: Business Intelligence

- Build decision intelligence on job, technician, and financial data
- Live dashboards: jobs per tech, first-time-fix rate, margin by job type/location, callback rates, estimator accuracy
- Anomaly alerts: location performance drops, rising parts costs
- Monthly intelligence briefs for first 90 days

### Stage 3: AI Services

- Intelligent scheduling & dynamic dispatching agents
- AI estimating/quoting system
- Technician mobile knowledge assistant (RAG + past jobs + manuals)
- Automated customer communication agents
- Advanced seasonal demand and capacity forecasting

### Stage 4: Continuous Improvement

- Monthly optimization of scheduling logic, estimating models, communication flows
- New opportunity scanning as business grows
- Quarterly strategy refresh

**Revenue story:** Audit > Intelligence layer > Build high-ROI systems > Keep improving forever. Once 2–3 strong HVAC case studies exist, selling the next one becomes much easier.

## Worked Example: Professional Services Firm

**Typical client:** Mid-sized accounting firm, law firm, or marketing agency (25–120 people), $5M–$25M revenue, partners doing too much low-leverage work, inconsistent delivery quality, slow proposals, weak knowledge sharing.

### Stage 1: Audit

- Map partner/manager time allocation, proposal process, research workflows, client delivery, knowledge management, pricing
- Identify opportunities: proposal automation, research agents, knowledge systems, delivery quality agents, pricing intelligence

### Stage 2: Business Intelligence

- Intelligence on realization rates, utilization, project profitability, proposal win rates, client concentration, partner productivity
- Automated monthly insights and anomaly detection

### Stage 3: AI Services

- AI research and memo/drafting agents
- Proposal and pricing automation
- Internal knowledge/expertise RAG system
- Client delivery quality and consistency agents
- Advanced scenario modeling and capacity planning

### Stage 4: Continuous Improvement

- Monthly optimization of live agents and systems
- Continuous capture of new firm knowledge
- Fresh opportunity identification
- Quarterly firm-performance and strategy review

**Revenue story:** Same ladder. Professional services often has higher average project values but slightly longer sales cycles and more stakeholder complexity than HVAC.

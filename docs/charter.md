## Core Idea

Build a personalized agentic factory — a system of AI agents organized like a real corporation — that autonomously builds, runs, and grows multiple companies in parallel, profitably and with minimal tooling.

The factory behaves like a complete autonomous company operating system, not a collection of isolated AI assistants.

**Not a single product.** A factory that produces and operates many businesses at once.
**Not a team of humans.** Every employee below the Founding Partner is an AI agent — available 24/7, scalable on demand.
**Not expensive.** Runs on bare-minimum tools and subscriptions.
**Not theoretical.** Every business targets real customers paying real money.

## Founder Context

The Founding Partner is a Computer Science major working extensively on Data and AI technologies at a major tech firm, with deep understanding of AI and agentic systems.

AI agents are already consuming ~4x more tokens than humans and performing real-world tasks — design, software development, consulting, digital product creation — better than humans in many cases. The opportunity: build an entire agentic company to develop ideas faster, build products quicker, and reach more customers at scale, across multiple businesses simultaneously.

## Inspiration

Conceptually similar to [OpenExecutive](https://github.com/SenteLabsAI/OpenExecutive) (a virtual C-suite with ~10 specialist agents), but with a distinct vision: a full factory model with layered hierarchy, shared C-Suite think tank, and parallel company verticals — not a single-company executive agent.

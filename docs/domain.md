## Product (DFY by Axiom)

**Stage 1: AI Opportunity Audit + 90-Day Roadmap**  
Diagnostic stage. We deeply analyze the client’s operations, data, and workflows to identify the highest-ROI AI and agentic opportunities, then deliver a clear, prioritized 90-day implementation roadmap with expected impact.

**Stage 2: AI Business Intelligence System**  
Intelligence layer stage. We design and stand up a practical AI-powered business intelligence and decision system, including core dashboards, automated insights, anomaly detection, and an initial period of monthly intelligence briefs so leadership can make better and faster decisions.

**Stage 3: AI-Powered Digital Services + Advanced Business Intelligence**  
Execution stage. We build and implement the actual AI agents, automation systems, digital tools, and advanced intelligence capabilities identified in earlier stages — turning strategy into working solutions that improve operations, revenue, or leverage.

**Stage 4: Continuous Improvement**  
Ongoing optimization stage. We provide a structured monthly retainer to continuously monitor, refine, and enhance all live AI systems and processes, surface new opportunities, and ensure the client’s advantage compounds over time rather than plateauing.

---

This framing keeps the progression clear: **Diagnose → Install Intelligence → Execute → Continuously Improve**.

### 1. Multi-location HVAC

**Typical client**: 8–25 location HVAC company, $8M–$40M revenue, 40–150 technicians, mix of residential + light commercial, struggling with scheduling, consistency, margins, and technician knowledge.

#### Stage 1: AI Opportunity Audit + 90-Day Roadmap
**What you do**:
- Deep discovery across dispatching, estimating, technician workflows, customer communication, parts, and job profitability
- Map current tech stack and data sources
- Identify 6–8 high-ROI opportunities (e.g., intelligent dispatching, AI estimating, technician knowledge assistant, automated follow-ups, job margin intelligence)
- Prioritize by impact vs. feasibility
- Deliver a clear 90-day roadmap with expected $ impact

**Client value**: Owner finally sees exactly where AI will make the most money and in what order.  
**Your revenue**: Premium fixed-fee diagnostic (strong five figures once proven).

#### Stage 2: AI Business Intelligence System
**What you do**:
- Build a practical decision intelligence layer on top of their job, technician, and financial data
- Live dashboards + automated insights on: jobs per tech, first-time-fix rate, margin by job type/location, callback rates, estimator accuracy, etc.
- Anomaly alerts (sudden drop in a location’s performance, rising parts cost, etc.)
- Monthly intelligence brief for the first 90 days

**Client value**: Owners and managers stop flying blind. They see real profitability and performance in near real-time.  
**Your revenue**: Project fee + path into retainer.

#### Stage 3: AI-Powered Digital Services + Advanced BI
**What you do** (examples):
- Intelligent scheduling & dynamic dispatching agents
- AI estimating / quoting system
- Technician mobile knowledge assistant (RAG + past jobs + manuals)
- Automated customer communication agents (reminders, status, reviews)
- Advanced forecasting (seasonal demand, capacity planning)
- Custom internal tools as needed

**Client value**: Actual operational improvement — more jobs completed, higher margins, less chaos.  
**Your revenue**: Larger project fees (implementation) + expansion revenue.

#### Stage 4: Continuous Improvement
**What you do**:
- Monthly review of all live systems
- Ongoing optimization of scheduling logic, estimating models, communication flows
- New opportunity scanning as the business grows or changes
- Quarterly strategy refresh
- Priority iteration support

**Client value**: The advantage keeps compounding instead of plateauing.  
**Your revenue**: High-margin monthly retainer (this becomes the long-term profit engine).

**Revenue story**: Audit → Intelligence layer → Build the high-ROI systems → Keep improving them forever. Once you have 2–3 strong HVAC case studies, selling the next one becomes much easier.

### 2. Professional Services Firms

**Typical client**: Mid-sized accounting firm, law firm, or marketing agency (25–120 people), $5M–$25M revenue, partners still doing too much low-leverage work, inconsistent delivery quality, slow proposal process, weak internal knowledge sharing.

#### Stage 1: AI Opportunity Audit + 90-Day Roadmap
**What you do**:
- Map partner/manager time allocation, proposal process, research workflows, client delivery, knowledge management, and pricing
- Identify highest-ROI opportunities (proposal automation, research agents, knowledge systems, delivery quality agents, pricing intelligence, etc.)
- Prioritized 90-day roadmap with clear time and margin impact

**Client value**: Partners see exactly how to buy back their time and improve firm leverage.  
**Your revenue**: Premium fixed-fee diagnostic.

#### Stage 2: AI Business Intelligence System
**What you do**:
- Build intelligence on realization rates, utilization, project profitability, proposal win rates, client concentration, partner productivity
- Automated monthly insights and anomaly detection
- Simple operating cadence so leadership actually uses it

**Client value**: The firm finally has clear economic visibility instead of gut feel.  
**Your revenue**: Project fee + retainer path.

#### Stage 3: AI-Powered Digital Services + Advanced BI
**What you do** (examples):
- AI research and memo/drafting agents
- Proposal and pricing automation systems
- Internal knowledge / expertise RAG system
- Client delivery quality and consistency agents
- Advanced scenario modeling and capacity planning
- Custom internal tools for the firm’s specific workflows

**Client value**: Real leverage — more output per partner/manager, faster delivery, better consistency.  
**Your revenue**: Larger implementation projects.

#### Stage 4: Continuous Improvement
**What you do**:
- Monthly optimization of the live agents and systems
- Continuous capture of new firm knowledge
- Fresh opportunity identification
- Quarterly firm-performance and strategy review

**Client value**: The firm keeps getting more leveraged over time instead of the AI systems going stale.  
**Your revenue**: Monthly retainer.

**Revenue story**: Same ladder — Diagnose → Install intelligence → Build leverage systems → Continuously improve. Professional services often has higher average project values but slightly longer sales cycles and more stakeholder complexity than HVAC.
